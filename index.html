How SaveLife 360 Works

Bridging the communication gap between citizens in distress, emergency ambulance drivers on the road, and hospital trauma centers.

STEP 01
👤

1. Citizen Requests

GPS coordinates are detected instantly with one click. Citizen specifies emergency type (Accident, Cardiac, Breathing) to mobilize specialized life-support equipment.

➔
STEP 02
🚑

2. Ambulance Dispatched

Nearest certified driver receives turn-by-turn navigation HUD and accepts. Live tracking and approaching road alerts broadcast to traffic. Paramedics secure patient.

➔
STEP 03
🏥

3. Hospital Prepares

Hospital ER receives vitals and incoming ETA before arrival. Emergency beds, doctors and surgical suites are reserved and ready when the ambulance arrives.

Key Capabilities

Modern technologies built to eliminate delays at every stage of medical transportation.

🚑

Ambulance Tracking

Real-time GPS tracking with continuous speed, heading, and distance calculation for zero guesswork.

📍

Live Location Detection

Browser Geolocation API pinpointing patient pickup locations down to street address level in seconds.

🗺️

Route Optimization

Intelligent hospital selection matching patient clinical condition with nearest capable ER facilities.

🚨

Emergency Road Alerts

Surrounding vehicles receive safe right-of-way proximity warnings to clear congestion ahead of ambulances.

🏥

Hospital Availability

Transparent, live counts of emergency department beds, ICU availability, and on-duty surgical teams.

🔔

Real-Time Notifications

In-app audio sirens, live status timeline shifts, and synchronized alerts keeping all stakeholders aligned.
